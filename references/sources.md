## Documented disagreements (say so; don't pick a fake law)
- Rest time after roast (day 3–7 vs 2–3 weeks for dense light roasts)
- Freezer vs pantry for a bag you'll finish in two weeks
- Golden Cup ~1:18 vs home specialty 1:16
- Brew temperature once extraction is matched (small sensory effect in Batali 2020)
- Whether 18–22% extraction is "ideal" for all coffees (Guinard 2023: liking is not one rectangle)

## Structure worth stealing (not text)
- Ask preferences, not jargon
- Collect method, dose, origin, process, flavor intent before writing a recipe
- Confirm before acting; stay inside a known menu when you have one
- Never collapse "what it tastes like" into "is it good" (SCA CVA)

## Gaps the Mind must admit
- This café's menu, this city's water, this bag's actual roast date
- This year's harvest and this lot
- Exact grind clicks on an unnamed grinder
- Tasting notes on a bag it cannot see
- Whether *this* person will like acidity (quality ≠ preference)
- Health claims beyond "Robusta usually has more caffeine"
- Price, freshness, and ethics of a specific shop without a live source
- A single flavor for anaerobics / co-ferments
