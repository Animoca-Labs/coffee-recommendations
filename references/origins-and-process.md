# Origins, process, roast

Use these as tendencies, not promises. Lot, variety, altitude, harvest, roast, water, and brew routinely override country stereotypes.

## Species
- **Arabica** (*C. arabica*): more aromatic complexity, more perceived acidity, less bitterness. Specialty default.
- **Robusta / canephora** (*C. canephora*): more body, more bitterness, roughly 2× caffeine. Fine Robusta is a real CQI category. Do not sneer. Vietnamese, Italian-blend, and some specialty canephora are legitimate.
- ICO coffee-year 2024 production was about 57% Arabica / 43% Robusta by bags. The 2016 SCA/WCR flavor wheel was built mostly on arabica samples; a Canephora flavor wheel was published 2025.

## Origin → typical cup

Process, variety, and roast often beat country. A natural Guji and a washed Guji usually differ more than two naturals from neighboring Ethiopian regions.

| Origin (typical process) | Typical cup | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Ethiopia washed (Yirgacheffe, Sidama, Guji) | Floral, citrus, bergamot/jasmine, tea-like, lighter body | Black coffee; people who like tea, perfume, brightness |
| Ethiopia natural (Guji, Sidama, Harrar) | Berry/jam, winey, heavier; blueberry is the cliché, not a guarantee | Fruit-lovers; polarizing if they hate "funky" |
| Kenya (usually washed / double-washed) | Very bright; blackcurrant, grapefruit; sometimes savory tomato | Punchy, wine-like structure — not beginners who fear sour |
| Rwanda / Burundi | Honeyed citrus, black tea, floral | Gentler "Africa" than Kenya |
| Colombia (historically washed; now anything) | Balanced: caramel, chocolate, apple/stone fruit | Safest "I just want good coffee" origin |
| Brazil (natural / pulped-natural) | Nut, milk chocolate, caramel, low acid, full body | Milk drinks, espresso blends, "not sour" drinkers |
| Guatemala / Costa Rica / Honduras | Cocoa, brown sugar, apple/spice; Costa Rica famous for honey process | Everyday specialty; good drip |
| Peru | Mild, clean, often chocolate/nut | Easy daily cup |
| Sumatra / much of Indonesia (**wet-hulled / giling basah**, not washed) | Heavy, earthy, herbal/spice, low acid, cedar/dark chocolate | "Bold, not bright"; polarizing |
| Yemen (traditional natural) | Dried fruit, spice, winey | Curious drinkers; expensive/scarce |
| Vietnam | Mostly Robusta; condensed-milk café culture | Strong, sweet milk drinks — not a flavor-wheel origin |

Do not invent notes for a specific bag. Read the roaster's card, then translate.

## Process → flavor

Processing = what happens to the fruit between pick and dry green bean. **Honey is not honey added** — it is sticky mucilage left on the seed.

| Process | What happens | Typical cup | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Washed (wet) | Skin + most fruit removed; mucilage fermented/washed off; dried in parchment | Cleaner, brighter, more origin/variety clarity, lighter body | Not automatically "better" |
| Natural (dry) | Whole cherry dried 2–4 weeks | Heavier body, ripe/dried fruit, more sweetness; can be winey | Higher defect risk if sloppy |
| Honey / pulped natural | Skin off, some mucilage left (yellow → red → black = more mucilage) | More sweetness/body than washed, more clarity than many naturals | Name is tactile, not flavor |
| Anaerobic | Sealed low-oxygen ferment of cherry or depulped seed; then finished as natural/honey/washed | Intense aromatics: candy, tropical, wine, spice | Describes ferment *environment*, not a single taste. Bad ones taste like process |
| Wet-hulled (giling basah) | Parchment removed while still ~30–50% moisture, then finish-dried | Signature Sumatra: heavy, earthy, muted acidity | **Not** the same as washed |
| Co-ferment | Fruit/spices added during ferment | Can taste like the added fruit | Disclose it; don't sell as terroir |

Rule of thumb: naturals → fruit + juice/body; washed → acidity + clarity.

## Roast → flavor

More roast ≠ more sweetness. Sweetness is a bell curve: too light = grassy/sour; peak = sweet; too dark = bitter/ashy as sugars are destroyed. Dark roast covers origin.

| Roast | What you taste | Brew note |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Origin, florals, fruit, brighter acids; tea-like body | Needs good extraction (often finer/hotter/longer) or it tastes sour |
| Medium | Caramel, chocolate, nut, still some origin | Most forgiving daily roast |
| Dark | Roast: smoke, bitter chocolate, toast; origins converge | Extracts faster — go coarser/cooler; oils on the bean stale faster |

Myths to kill gently:
- Darker is not "stronger." Strength is ratio / TDS.
- Darker is not reliably more caffeine. Caffeine is fairly heat-stable; a *scoop* of dark roast can have less because beans lose mass and puff.
